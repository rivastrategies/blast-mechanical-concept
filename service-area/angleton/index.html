Start with the symptom

AC problems we diagnose

The same symptom can have several causes. Testing determines what is actually responsible before a repair is recommended.

Warm or weak airflow

The system runs, but rooms stay warm or airflow at the vents feels weak.

System will not start

The thermostat calls for cooling, but the indoor or outdoor equipment does not operate.

Short cycling

The system repeatedly starts and stops without maintaining comfortable conditions.

Ice or water

Ice appears on the refrigerant line or water collects near the indoor equipment.

Unusual sounds or odors

New mechanical noise, electrical odor, or excessive vibration deserves attention.

Breaker trips

A repeated trip can indicate an electrical or equipment fault and should not be reset continuously.
