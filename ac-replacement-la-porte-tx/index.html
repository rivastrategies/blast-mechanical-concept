Repair or Replace? How to Decide

There comes a point where repairing an old system stops making financial sense. If your unit is aging out, needs frequent repairs, or can't keep up with Texas summers anymore, replacement is worth a serious look. We'll give you an honest comparison of repair cost vs. replacement cost before you spend a dollar on either.

Our Replacement Process

  1. Free in-home quote and load calculation with proper sizing, not just matching the old unit
  2. Clear system options and pricing, explained in plain terms
  3. Professional installation, typically completed in a single day

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a new AC system instead of a repair?

As a general rule, if your system is over 10-12 years old and facing a major repair, replacement usually saves money over the long run through lower energy bills and fewer breakdowns. We'll walk you through the real numbers for your specific system before you decide.

How long does AC replacement take?

Most residential replacements are completed in a single day. We'll confirm a specific timeline for your home during the quote visit.

Do you help me pick the right size system?

Yes -- proper sizing is one of the most common mistakes in the industry. We calculate load requirements based on your home's actual size, insulation, and layout rather than just matching the old unit.
