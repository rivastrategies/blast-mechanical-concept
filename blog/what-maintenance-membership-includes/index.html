What Does a Home HVAC Maintenance Membership Actually Include?

Blast Mechanical's $300 annual maintenance membership includes two seasonal tune-ups, priority scheduling, 10% to 15% repair savings, a waived diagnostic fee, and credit toward a future system replacement while the membership remains active.

Two Tune-Ups Each Year

The plan includes one spring tune-up and one fall tune-up. These visits are designed to prepare the system for the seasons when heating and cooling demand changes most.

Priority Service and Repair Savings

Active members receive priority scheduling, a waived diagnostic fee, and 10% to 15% off HVAC repairs.

Credit Toward a Future Replacement

Membership credit accumulates toward a qualifying future system replacement while the homeowner remains enrolled. Blast Mechanical will confirm the available credit when replacement planning begins.
