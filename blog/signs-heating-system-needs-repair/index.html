Signs Your Heating System Needs Repair Before Winter

Uneven heating, unusual smells or noises, short cycling, and rising energy bills are the clearest signs your heating system needs a repair visit before cold weather arrives.

Southeast Texas winters are mild compared to most of the country, but cold snaps do happen -- and a heating system that's been sitting unused for months is more likely to reveal a problem right when you need it most.

Don't Wait for a Cold Snap to Find Out

A pre-season check catches issues while there's still time to fix them on your schedule, not during an emergency call in the middle of a cold front.
