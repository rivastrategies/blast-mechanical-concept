Indoor Air Quality and Humidity in the Houston/Galveston Climate

Gulf Coast humidity makes indoor air quality management more important than in drier climates -- excess moisture contributes to mold growth and allergen buildup if not addressed alongside your HVAC system.

Homes in the Houston and Galveston County area contend with humidity levels that most of the country doesn't deal with. An HVAC system alone isn't always enough to manage it -- especially in older homes or homes with larger square footage.

What This Means for Your Home

Persistent musty odors, visible condensation, or allergy symptoms that worsen indoors can all point to a humidity or air quality issue worth addressing directly.
