What a Seasonal Heating Visit Checks

The exact checklist depends on your equipment. A visit can include thermostat operation, filters and airflow, blower condition, electrical controls, temperature response and heating safety controls. Furnace checks may include ignition, flame sensing and venting; heat pump checks may include defrost and auxiliary heat.

Maintenance or a Heating Problem?

If your heater is not starting or is blowing cold air, tell us when you call. We can discuss a heating repair visit so your request reaches the right service path.

Seasonal Service and Annual Membership Are Different

You can contact us about seasonal heating service. If you want ongoing care, ask about the $300 annual Blast Comfort Membership, which includes one spring cooling visit and one fall heating visit. The annual membership price is not a quoted price for an individual tune-up. Repairs are not included in the annual price.
