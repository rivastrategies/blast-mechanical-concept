About Blast Mechanical

Blast Mechanical is a residential HVAC contractor founded on June 6, 2026 and based at 128 South 16th Street, La Porte, TX. We serve homeowners across East Houston/Harris County, the Clear Lake/NASA Corridor, and mainland Galveston County.

Founding Partner Armando V. Bueno leads a team with 35 years of hands-on home-service experience. Blast Mechanical is a new company built to bring those established technical standards to residential AC repair, heating repair, system replacement, maintenance, and indoor air quality service.

Blast Mechanical is fully insured, holds Texas HVAC License TACLA137109C, and is enrolled with the Better Business Bureau and Air Conditioning Contractors of America.
