# Angleton Google Search pilot

Prepared September 8, 2026. Status: **ready for account build; keep paused**.

## Approved media envelope

- Campaign: `Search | Angleton | AC Repair | Pilot`
- Campaign type: Search only
- Budget type: campaign total budget
- Campaign total: $750
- Flight: 15 consecutive days, with exact dates selected at activation
- Bidding: Maximize conversions
- Final URL: `https://blast-mech.com/service-area/angleton/`
- Geography: Angleton, Texas city target
- Location option: Presence — people in or regularly in the targeted location
- Language: English
- Search Partners: off initially
- Display Network: off
- Auto-tagging: on
- Final URL suffix: `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=angleton_ac_repair_pilot&utm_content={creative}&utm_term={keyword}`

Google documents campaign total budgets for new Search campaigns with a fixed start and end date. The budget type cannot be changed after creation, and current Google guidance says bulk support for Search total budgets is not yet available. Create the campaign shell manually, then paste or import the included keywords, negatives, and ads.

1. Publish and test the Angleton page and generic HCP confirmation route.
2. Confirm a real HCP test submission arrives in Job Inbox and Pipeline.
3. Confirm the form redirects only after successful submission.
4. Resolve whether `281-506-4822` or `(281) 941-4431` is the correct tracked advertising number.

6. Link or import the confirmed `generate_lead` event into Google Ads.
7. Build the campaign as paused and verify every setting in `launch-checklist.md`.
8. Activate only after explicit authorization to fund/spend.

## Commercial scorecard

- Qualified lead acquisition cost = $750 / qualified leads
- Customer acquisition cost = $750 / booked new customers
- ROAS = attributable completed-job revenue / $750
- Break-even booked jobs = $750 / average contribution dollars per completed job, rounded up

Clicks, impressions, CTR, and page views are diagnostics. They are not success outcomes.
